# Research

This folder holds NanoClaw research notes that connect the project to adjacent
open-source ecosystems.

## Current Tracks

- `RWA_AGENTIC_PAYMENTS_WATCHLIST.md`: public GitHub map of tokenized
  collateral, RWA, decentralized AI, and agentic payment projects to watch.
- `RWA_AGENTIC_PAYMENTS_SCENARIOS.md`: seven probabilistic scenarios for how
  those signals could affect NanoClaw, provenance workflows, collector access,
  and future payment boundaries.

## Boundary

These notes are research and documentation. They do not mint NFTs, connect
wallets, move funds, create investment products, or imply partnership with any
listed organization.
